TWEEN
Shows your PlayStation (or any console) full screen on your Mac, sharper and smoother.
Works with an HDMI capture card, or with the PS Remote Play app.

Needs: a Mac with macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer. Best on Apple Silicon (M1 or newer).


1. INSTALL
   Drag Tween into your Applications folder.


2. OPEN IT THE FIRST TIME
   Tween isn't from the App Store, so macOS blocks it the first time. This is expected.

   a. Double-click Tween. macOS says it can't verify the app. Click Done (not Move to Bin).
   b. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
   c. Scroll down. Next to "Tween was blocked", click Open Anyway, then enter your Mac password.
   d. Click Open. From now on it opens normally.


3. PICK WHERE THE PICTURE COMES FROM
   Tween opens full screen. Press Tab to open its settings, and under Source choose:

   - Capture card: for an HDMI capture card plugged into your Mac.
     Allow Camera (the video) and Microphone (the game sound) when asked.

   - Remote Play app: for the PS Remote Play app on this Mac (no capture card needed).
     Start streaming in PS Remote Play first. Allow Screen Recording when asked,
     then click Quit & Reopen. Tween finds the Remote Play window by itself.
     Keep PS Remote Play open; it can sit behind Tween. Game sound comes from Remote Play.


4. KEYS
   Tab   settings
   O     stats: off / compact / full
   U     sharper upscaling on / off
   S     sharpening on / off
   F     smooth 60 fps on / off (macOS 26 or newer; adds about 0.04 s of delay, so leave it off for fast games)
   V     V-Sync on / off (off = less delay, may tear)
   M     fit / fill / stretch
   P     take a screenshot (saved in Pictures > Tween)
   R     start / stop recording, with sound (saved in Movies > Tween)
   N     microphone on / off while you are live
   ⌘Q    quit


5. GO LIVE ON YOUTUBE OR TWITCH
   Press Tab and scroll to Live stream. Turn on YouTube, Twitch or both, paste the stream key
   from the site, and click Go live. Click End stream to stop. No key on the keyboard starts a stream.

   - Viewers see the picture Tween shows, with the game sound. You can add your microphone and
     a small webcam picture in a corner; the webcam is never shown on your own screen.
   - Set the title and who can watch on YouTube or Twitch itself.
   - Your stream key is saved on this Mac in a file only your account can read. It is not encrypted.
   - With a capture card, viewers hear the game only while "Play the capture card's sound" is on.
   - If the game feels slower while you are live with F on, set In-between frames to Fast.


6. TIPS
   - For the least delay, leave F off and connect your PlayStation and Mac by ethernet cable.
   - If the picture freezes in Remote Play mode, move the PS Remote Play window to another screen.
   - If something says access is off: System Settings > Privacy & Security, find the right
     section (Camera, Microphone or Screen & System Audio Recording), turn Tween on, and reopen it.
